Real Results — Examples & Excerpts

Sample exercise (excerpt)

Map 3 recurring symbols from your life. For each: note first memory, emotional valence, and the story you tell about it. Rotate the symbol through the 4 axes of identity to reframe meaning.

Visual example

A completed map might show how one symbol shifts from “shame” → “fuel” when recontextualized across the phoenix-cycle. This is how small reframes produce noticeable changes in behavior and self-talk.
